# Why Small Systems Fail in Big Ways

## Summary

- This source argues that many system failures come from ordinary assumptions interacting under stress rather than from one catastrophic bug. Source: raw/articles/sample-article-1.md
- It highlights assumptions about clock accuracy, harmless retries, and ordered delivery as examples that may break down as traffic grows or networks become unreliable. Source: raw/articles/sample-article-1.md
- It frames reliability as an ongoing design habit focused on making failure visible and survivable. Source: raw/articles/sample-article-1.md

## Key Points

- Partial failure and delayed or ambiguous communication should be expected in system design. Source: raw/articles/sample-article-1.md
- Reliability is presented as a practice, not a single feature. Source: raw/articles/sample-article-1.md
