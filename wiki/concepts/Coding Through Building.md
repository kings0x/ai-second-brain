# Coding Through Building

## Summary

- Coding improvement is linked to building real things rather than relying only on abstract study. Source: [[Sample Personal Note]]
- Systems design learning is more useful when tied back to coding practice. Source: [[Granola Meeting Export]]

## Key Points

- Small, concrete builds may be a better path than ambitious but vague projects. Source: [[Granola Meeting Export]]
- This idea complements the existing strategy of turning learning into outputs. Source: [[Sample Personal Note]], [[Claude Conversation Export]]
