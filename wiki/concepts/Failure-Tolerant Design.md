# Failure-Tolerant Design

## Summary

- Failure-tolerant design means building systems that expect delayed messages, partial failure, and ambiguous state rather than assuming ideal conditions. Source: [[Why Small Systems Fail in Big Ways]]

## Key Points

- The goal is not to eliminate all failures but to make them visible and survivable. Source: [[Why Small Systems Fail in Big Ways]]
- Reliability emerges from design habits and operational awareness, not from one isolated feature. Source: [[Why Small Systems Fail in Big Ways]]
