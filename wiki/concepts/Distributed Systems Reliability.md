# Distributed Systems Reliability

## Summary

- Reliability in distributed systems involves designing for conditions where common assumptions may fail under load or network instability. Source: [[Why Small Systems Fail in Big Ways]]
- The article source emphasizes that reliability is a habit of making failures visible and survivable. Source: [[Why Small Systems Fail in Big Ways]]

## Key Points

- Assumptions about close clocks, harmless retries, and in-order delivery can fail in practice. Source: [[Why Small Systems Fail in Big Ways]]
- Reliability work should account for delayed messages, partial failure, and ambiguity. Source: [[Why Small Systems Fail in Big Ways]]
