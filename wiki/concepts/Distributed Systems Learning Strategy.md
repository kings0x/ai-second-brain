# Distributed Systems Learning Strategy

## Summary

- One suggested learning strategy is to study one distributed systems topic at a time and turn it into a concrete output. Source: [[Claude Conversation Export]]
- Recommended starting topics are replication, consistency, partitioning, queues, and failure recovery. Source: [[Claude Conversation Export]]
- The broader goal is not just interview preparation but deeper understanding over time. Source: [[Sample Personal Note]]

## Key Points

- Turning learning into explainers, demo projects, or writeups may improve retention and usefulness. Source: [[Claude Conversation Export]]
- Keeping a running glossary can support steady understanding over time. Source: [[Claude Conversation Export]]
- Weekly study structure is still an open question. Source: [[Notion Export]]
- Systems learning should connect back to coding practice. Source: [[Granola Meeting Export]]
